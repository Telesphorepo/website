Add accounts for your team, give each person the repositories and server permissions they need, and choose how they sign in: with a password and a second step, or with the single sign-on of your company.

## Add people and give permissions

<Place where="In Telesphorepo" />

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** › **People**, and click **New account**.

2. Enter a **Username** and a **Password**, and click **Create account**. The page of the new person opens.

3. On that page, under **Repositories**, click **Give access**. A dialog opens.

4. Click each repository to move it to **Giving access**, then tick **Download** or **Publish** under **Permissions for new repositories**. **Download** is ticked already.

5. Click **Give access to 1 repository**. The button counts the repositories you chose.

</Steps>

<Done>Done when the repository is listed under **Repositories** on the page of the person.</Done>

The other repository permissions are **Delete files**, **Edit settings**, **Manage access** and **Manage CI pipelines**. To give several people access to one repository, use its **Access** tab, as in [Repositories and access](/repositories-and-access/#give-people-download-or-publish). To add people from the command line, see [user add](/server-reference/#user-add).

### Give server permissions

Server permissions decide who can change settings, people, storage and the rest of the server. A person with none uses only the repositories they are given.

<Place where="In Telesphorepo, on the page of the person" />

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** › **People**, and click the person.

2. Under **Server permissions**, choose a role.

   | Role | What it gives |
   |---|---|
   | **Member** | No server permissions |
   | **Administrator** | Every server permission |
   | **Custom** | One switch per permission, each with what it opens |

3. Click **Save** in the bar at the bottom, then **Save permissions** in the dialog.

</Steps>

<Done>Done when the bar is gone and the page shows the role you chose.</Done>

You can give only the permissions that you hold.

## Set up two-step verification

A person needs a second step to sign in when they hold any permission other than **Download** and **Publish**. Everyone needs one to approve a computer or make a publish token. A single sign-on account has no second step, and it approves a computer with a new single sign-on.

<Place where="In Telesphorepo, on the page of your own account" />

<Steps>

1. Open the menu of your account in the top bar, click **Your account**, then click **Sign-in and security**.

2. Under **Two-step verification**, click **Add** on the **Authenticator app** row or the **Passkey** row, and follow the page.

3. Under **Recovery codes**, click **Generate codes**. Keep the codes in a safe place.

</Steps>

<Done>Done when the page says **Two-step verification is on**.</Done>

## Single sign-on

People can sign in with an OpenID Connect provider, such as Keycloak, Entra ID, Okta or Google. Anyone at the provider can sign in, and the first sign-in makes an account with no permissions. You give permissions on the **People** page, as for any account, or let the provider decide them as described below.

<Place where="At your provider, then in Telesphorepo" />

<Steps>

1. At your provider, add an app with the return address `https://repo.example.com/api/auth/oidc/callback`. Write your own address in place of `repo.example.com`. The **Return address** field on the **Sign-in options** page shows this address.

</Steps>

<Place where="In Telesphorepo" />

<Steps>

2. Open **Settings** › **Sign-in options**.

3. Under **Connect your provider**, enter the **Provider address**, the **App ID** and the **App secret** that your provider shows for the app.

4. Click **Test your edits**. The button reads **Test connection** when nothing is unsaved. A line says **Connected to** and the address of the provider when it works.

5. Click **Save** in the bar at the bottom.

</Steps>

<Done>Done when the sign-in page shows a **Continue with single sign-on** button.</Done>

### Permissions from the provider

To let the provider decide the server permissions, put the people in groups there and name the groups in Telesphorepo. Each sign-in then sets the permissions of the person from their groups, and the **People** page shows them read-only. Repository permissions stay on the server.

<Place where="At your provider, then in Telesphorepo" />

<Steps>

1. At your provider, make a group for each role, such as `repo-admins`. Put the groups in a field of the ID token, such as `groups`.

</Steps>

<Place where="In Telesphorepo, on Sign-in options" />

<Steps>

2. Under **Permissions from the provider**, enter that field in **Group field**.

3. Under **Permissions by group**, click **Add a group**. A form opens.

4. Enter the **Group**, choose its role, and click **Add group**.

5. Click **Save** in the bar at the bottom.

</Steps>

<Done>Done when the group is listed under **Permissions by group**.</Done>

### Make managers use single sign-on

On the **Sign-in options** page, under **How people sign in**, set **Password sign-in** to **Only people who publish; people who manage use single sign-on**, and click **Save**. People who only download and publish keep their password, and a manager signs in with single sign-on once first.

## Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary>Someone lost every second factor</summary>

On the machine of the server:

```sh
docker exec -it telesphorepo /telesphorepo-server user reset-2fa alice
```

The person sets up a new factor at the next sign-in.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Someone forgot the password</summary>

On the page of the person, click **Reset password**. Or on the machine of the server:

```sh
docker exec -it telesphorepo /telesphorepo-server user passwd alice
```

</details>

<details>
<summary>Passkeys stopped working</summary>

A passkey belongs to the host of the server address. When the host changes, each person adds a new passkey. Set the **Public base URL** on **Settings** › **General** before people add passkeys.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Single sign-on broke, and managers cannot sign in</summary>

Start the server with `-e TR_SIGNIN_PASSWORD_LOGIN=all`. Every local account can then sign in with a password again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Keycloak: the groups field</summary>

Add a **Group Membership** mapper to the client scope. Turn off **Full group path**, so the value is `repo-admins` and not `/repo-admins`. Enter `groups` in **Group field**. For Keycloak realm roles, enter `realm_access.roles` instead.

</details>
